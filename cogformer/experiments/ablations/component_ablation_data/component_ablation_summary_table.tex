\caption{Summary of component ablation variants: architectural role of each component,
the ablation applied, and the observed effect on posterior approximation quality
averaged across all five design conditions.
Parenthetical deltas refer to mean $\Delta$ relative to the baseline CogFormer
(see Table~\ref{tab:component_ablation_avg_delta}).}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}l >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X}
\toprule
Variant & Component Role & Observed Effect \\
\midrule
No SAB
  & Self-attention across parameter tokens; allows parameters to coordinate
    with one another during the denoising pass.
  & Marginal degradation on all metrics ($\Delta$NRMSE $= {+0.003}$,
    $\Delta$Post.\ Contr.\ $= {-0.007}$). The effect is small and
    inconsistent across conditions, indicating SAB provides only a modest
    inductive benefit. \\[4pt]
No MAB
  & Cross-attention from parameter tokens to the encoder summary; the primary
    pathway through which the decoder reads the observed data.
  & Catastrophic collapse of posterior contraction across all conditions
    ($\Delta$Post.\ Contr.\ $= {-0.839}$, $\Delta$NRMSE $= {+0.178}$).
    Without MAB, the decoder is effectively blind to the data, and the
    near-zero $\Delta$Cal.\ Error is an artifact of degenerate, uninformative
    posteriors rather than genuine calibration. \\[4pt]
No FiLM
  & Feature-wise linear modulation (FiLM) of the output projection,
    conditioned on the current diffusion timestep; scales and shifts the
    final representation before predicting velocity.
  & Small consistent improvement in recovery and posterior contraction
    ($\Delta$NRMSE $= {-0.005}$, $\Delta$Post.\ Contr.\ $= {+0.009}$).
    Removing FiLM slightly reduces noise in the output stage, suggesting the
    time-conditioned modulation introduces marginal over-parameterisation at
    this scale. \\[4pt]
No Fourier
  & Learnable Fourier random-feature embedding of the diffusion timestep
    \citep{tancik2020fourier}; projects the scalar $t$ into a
    high-dimensional frequency space before concatenation.
  & Consistent small improvement in NRMSE and posterior contraction
    ($\Delta$NRMSE $= {-0.006}$, $\Delta$Post.\ Contr.\ $= {+0.006}$)
    at the cost of slightly worse calibration ($\Delta$Cal.\ Error $= {+0.003}$).
    The learnable frequency projection offers no meaningful advantage over a
    fixed sinusoidal embedding for this task. \\
